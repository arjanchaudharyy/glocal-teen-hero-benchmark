How it works

Methodology & honesty

Every person is scored on the record they held when they were a Glocal honoree - the teen record the jury evaluated - not the career they built afterward. Comparing a current 15-year-old to someone's later university or startup years would be apples-to-oranges, so we don't. Each profile keeps a “now” note for where they went.

Seven dimensions

Where scores come from

Deep public research per person (press, the program's pages, LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolios). Higher-footprint honorees are well sourced; low-footprint ones get conservative estimates, flagged est. Weights live in the repo - change them and re-run.

A self-assessment tool built by a 2026 applicant. Scores reflect public information and a documented rubric, not the jury's decision. Not affiliated with Glocal Pvt. Ltd.
