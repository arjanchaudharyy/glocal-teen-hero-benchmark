How it works

Methodology

The seven dimensions

Drawn straight from the program's stated values (leadership, innovation & technology, social impact, creativity, community contribution, "beyond academics") and the organizer's own write-ups of why past winners won. Weighted, summing to 1.0:

    Scoring

    Your inputs map to a 0–5 score per dimension; the weighted total is compared to the winners' distribution to place you. The corpus scores are one careful reading of public information, not objective truth. Reproducible Python version and the full dataset live in the GitHub repo.
