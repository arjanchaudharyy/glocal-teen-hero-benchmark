How it works

Methodology & honesty

At-selection scoring

Everyone is scored on the record they held when they were a Glocal honoree — the teen record the jury evaluated — not the career they built afterward. That's the only fair basis for “would I be selected.” Comparing a current 15-year-old to someone's post-award university or startup years would be apples-to-oranges, so we don't.

The seven dimensions

    Where the scores come from

    Deep public research per person (press, the program's pages, LinkedIn/GitHub/portfolios). Winners and higher-footprint honorees are well-sourced; low-footprint honorees get conservative estimates and are flagged est. The weights live in the repo — change them and re-run.

    Self-assessment tool built by a 2026 applicant. Scores reflect public info + a documented rubric, not the jury's decision. Not affiliated with Glocal Pvt. Ltd. Corpus facts belong to their sources.
